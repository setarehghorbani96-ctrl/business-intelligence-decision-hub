# Demo Guide

Use this flow for a concise portfolio walkthrough of the Executive Command Center.

## Recommended Demo Flow

1. Open the Executive Dashboard at http://localhost:8501.
2. Start with all regions selected to show the full executive picture across NovaEnergy Services.
3. Compare North-West and North-East to highlight how revenue concentration and risk differ across the two strongest regions.
4. Use the monthly revenue and risk-related visuals to explain how management can spot performance gaps over time.
5. Add Islands into the comparison to show how a smaller region can still surface operational or ESG pressure points.
6. Review the recommendations table and explain how it reflects the currently selected regional scope.
7. Close on business value: leaders can compare regions quickly, prioritize interventions, and keep FastAPI as the governed KPI access layer.
