# Demo Guide

Use this 5-minute script for a polished walkthrough of the Executive Command Center.

## 5-Minute Demo Script

1. Open the dashboard at http://localhost:8501 and frame it as the executive decision layer for NovaEnergy Services.
2. Explain the business context: FastAPI exposes governed KPI views so leadership can monitor performance without querying operational systems directly.
3. Walk through the executive KPI cards to summarize revenue, margin, service quality, customer sentiment, downtime, emissions, health score, and risk.
4. Compare North-West and North-East to show how the dashboard surfaces trade-offs between revenue strength, risk exposure, and operational execution.
5. Add Islands into the comparison to show how a smaller region can still become a management priority because of service pressure or ESG performance.
6. Review the recommendations section and explain how the table prioritizes rule-based management actions from KPI thresholds.
7. Close on the end-to-end architecture: PostgreSQL KPI views feed FastAPI endpoints, and Streamlit turns that governed data layer into a demo-ready executive interface.
